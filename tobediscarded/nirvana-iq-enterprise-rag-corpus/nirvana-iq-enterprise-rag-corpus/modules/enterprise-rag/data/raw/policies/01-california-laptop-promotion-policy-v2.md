# California Laptop Promotion Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the seasonal laptop-discount campaign for Nirvana Retail Group stores and online orders delivered within California.

## Eligibility
The promotion applies to laptop products in the `COMPUTING-LAPTOPS` category. Clearance items, refurbished devices, and business-account bulk orders are excluded.

## Promotion Period
The campaign begins on January 1, 2026 and ends at 11:59 PM Pacific Time on March 31, 2026.

## Discount
Eligible products receive a 12% promotional discount.

## Marketing Support
Paid search, email, and in-store signage run throughout the campaign and stop when the promotion expires unless an extension is approved.

## Post-Campaign Rule
All promotional discounts and campaign advertisements must stop after March 31, 2026 unless the Vice President of Sales approves a written extension.
