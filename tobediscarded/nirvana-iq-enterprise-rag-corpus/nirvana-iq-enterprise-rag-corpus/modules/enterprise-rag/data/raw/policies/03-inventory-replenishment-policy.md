# Inventory Replenishment Policy

## Standard Thresholds
- Reorder point: 100 sellable units per regional warehouse
- Safety stock: 75 sellable units
- Critical stock: fewer than 50 sellable units
- Target post-replenishment level: 250 sellable units

## Laptop Category Rule
For laptops, Supply Planning must create a replenishment request when projected available inventory will fall below 100 units within the next 14 days.

## Promotion Rule
Before a regional promotion begins, inventory must cover forecast demand plus 20% safety capacity.

## Escalation
Critical stock during an active campaign must be escalated within four business hours.
