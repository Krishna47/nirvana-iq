# Electronics Return and Refund Policy

## Standard Return Window
Unopened electronics may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.

## Opened Laptops
Opened laptops may be returned within 15 calendar days of delivery and must include the charger and accessories.

## Defective Products
Verified defective laptops may be returned within 30 calendar days.

## Restocking Fee
A 10% restocking fee may apply to opened, non-defective laptops.
