# Supplier Delay Response SOP

## Trigger
This procedure applies when a confirmed shipment will arrive seven or more days late.

## Required Actions
1. Procurement records the revised delivery date.
2. Supply Planning recalculates projected inventory.
3. Sales Operations is notified when a promotion may be affected.
4. Marketing pauses campaigns when projected stock will fall below the critical threshold.

## Laptop Suppliers
Delays longer than 14 days require an executive exception review.
