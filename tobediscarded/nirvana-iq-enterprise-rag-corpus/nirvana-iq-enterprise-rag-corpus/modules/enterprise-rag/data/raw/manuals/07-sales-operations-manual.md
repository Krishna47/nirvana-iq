# Sales Operations Manual

## Weekly Performance Review
Regional teams review revenue, units, margin, conversion, returns, promotions, and stock availability every Monday.

## Decline Investigation
A month-over-month decline greater than 10% requires investigation of:
- expired promotions
- low-stock periods
- price changes
- return-rate changes
- supplier delays
- marketing activity

## Evidence Standard
Conclusions must identify whether they are supported by data, policy, operational records, or analyst judgment.
