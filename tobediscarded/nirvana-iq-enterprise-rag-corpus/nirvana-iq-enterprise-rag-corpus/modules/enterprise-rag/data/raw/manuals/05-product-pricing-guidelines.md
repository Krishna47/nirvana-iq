# Product Pricing Guidelines

## Price Floors
Promotional prices must not reduce gross margin below 8% without Finance approval.

## Promotion Review
Campaign pricing must be reviewed before launch and within five business days after completion.

## Laptop Category
Laptop promotions should be evaluated using unit sales, conversion, return rate, supplier funding, and inventory availability.

## Unauthorized Discounts
Store teams must not continue expired campaigns.
