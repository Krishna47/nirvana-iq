# Compliance Procedure — Texas #391

## Executive Summary

This document, NRG-SOP-GOLD-0391, outlines the compliance procedure applicable to the state of Texas for the product **Nirvana Smart Oven** (product code **NRG-HOM-2001**). The procedure is aimed at ensuring all statutory and regulatory requirements are met through rigorous sampling and testing protocols. The sampling for week number **28** involves **111** units selected for quality and compliance verification. The procedure is governed by the Compliance department and has been approved for implementation as of February 14, 2026.

---

## Summary

The compliance procedure NRG-SOP-GOLD-0391 defines the steps and controls required to validate regulatory adherence of the Nirvana Smart Oven (**NRG-HOM-2001**) within the Texas region. The process includes sampling a defined number of units, testing for safety, functionality, and documentation completeness, followed by reporting and corrective action if needed. This procedure ensures consistent compliance across all distribution points in Texas.

---

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure ensures that all Nirvana Smart Oven products (product code **NRG-HOM-2001**) marketed and sold in Texas meet applicable legal and company standards. It applies exclusively to the Texas region and includes all retail, warehouse, and distribution facilities operating under Nirvana Retail Group within this jurisdiction.

Scope includes:

- Sampling and testing during week number **28** each calendar year
- Inspection of **111** sample units selected randomly
- Documentation review and verification processes
- Handling and reporting of non-conformances and deviations

---

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Nirvana Smart Oven shipment and inventory records for Texas.
- Availability of qualified personnel from the Compliance department.
- Testing equipment calibrated and validated per company standards.
- Updated regulatory checklists reflecting Texas-specific statutes.

---

## Procedure Steps

1. **Sample Selection**  
   During week number **28**, select **111** Nirvana Smart Oven units at random from Texas distribution centers and retail locations.

2. **Verification of Documentation**  
   Confirm all devices are accompanied by correct manuals, warranty cards, and compliance certifications.

3. **Product Testing**  
   Conduct electrical safety tests, performance benchmarks, and firmware verification on each unit sampled.

4. **Data Recording**  
   Document all test results using the Compliance department’s standard logs.

5. **Non-Compliance Handling**  
   If any sample fails testing, immediately escalate following the Exceptions and Escalation procedure.

6. **Reporting**  
   Compile a compliance report indicating outcomes, deviations, and corrective actions taken.

7. **Record Retention**  
   Retain all documentation for a minimum of three years as per company policy.

---

## Controls

- Sampling size fixed at **111** units for accuracy and representativeness.
- Testing strictly adheres to regulatory standards for Texas.
- Segregation of duties among sample selectors, testers, and record keepers to prevent conflicts of interest.
- Periodic calibration of testing instruments ensured quarterly.

---

## Controls and Metrics

- Compliance pass rate target: 98% of sampled units per week number **28**.
- Number of deviations logged and resolved within 15 business days.
- Documentation completeness rate maintained at 100%.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Compliance Department**: Primary owner of the procedure; responsible for execution, monitoring, and reporting.
- **Quality Assurance Team**: Conducts testing and validation of samples.
- **Logistics Coordinators**: Facilitate sample retrieval and documentation access.
- **Regional Manager, Texas**: Oversees adherence to the procedure within all facilities in Texas.

---

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to sampling size or procedure timing must be approved in writing by the Compliance Director. Deviations found in sample testing require immediate notification to the Regional Manager and escalation to Corporate Compliance if unresolved within 10 business days. Documentation of all exceptions is mandatory.

## Review Cycle

This procedure, version **1.0**, is reviewed annually, prior to week number **28**, or immediately following significant regulatory updates impacting Texas. Revisions are approved by the Compliance Director to maintain status as **approved**.
