## Controls and Metrics  
- Weekly sampling control aligned to **106** units for the Nirvana Gamer X product data feeds.  
- Automated alerts on any deviation below 95% of the sample_units threshold.  
- Continuous validation through pipeline monitoring tools ensuring integrity and processing speed targets for the Washington region.  
- Version control and audit trails mandated on all data engineering configurations supporting NRG-LAP-1004.

## Roles and Responsibilities  
- **Data Engineering Team:** Implement and maintain data pipelines conforming to this policy; conduct weekly sampling and validation with required sample_unit thresholds.  
- **Data Quality Analysts:** Monitor metrics and generate compliance reports on sample units, ensuring all thresholds meet or exceed **106** as defined for key review weeks including **week_number 11**.

## Exceptions and Escalation  
Exceptions to any data sample size or control must be submitted in writing with a justification document to the Data Engineering Manager. Critical deviations impacting operational integrity require immediate escalation to the regional leadership and Data Governance Board. Temporary waivers may be granted only under extraordinary circumstances and for limited durations.

## Review Cycle  
This policy shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes to the Nirvana Gamer X product data streams, regional data infrastructure, or business requirements—whichever occurs first. The Data Engineering department in Washington is responsible for initiating the review process, with final approval from the Regional Compliance Officer.
