# Electronics Return and Refund Policy

## Executive Summary

This document—NRG-POL-CX-004—defines the Electronics Return and Refund Policy for the Customer Experience department in the United States region. The policy outlines the return window, exclusions, and refund procedures specifically for electronic products. The version 2.0 policy is currently approved and effective from November 1, 2025. Its purpose is to establish clear guidelines to ensure consistent customer handling while protecting company interests.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this policy is to provide a standardized procedure for returns and refunds of electronic items sold by Nirvana Retail Group within the United States. It applies to all electronics purchased through Nirvana Retail Group retail outlets and online channels. This policy ensures compliance with company standards and enhances customer satisfaction by defining transparent timelines and conditions under which electronics can be returned or refunded.

## Purpose

To set forth the requirements, limitations, and procedures for returning electronic products and obtaining refunds. This policy aims to balance customer rights with operational efficiency and risk mitigation for Nirvana Retail Group.

## Return Window

The electronics return window is strictly defined as **15 days** from the date of purchase or delivery receipt. Customers must initiate and complete any return within this 15-day period. Returns requested beyond **15 days** cannot be accepted under this policy.

- **Example:** An opened non-defective laptop **cannot** be returned after 20 days since the purchase, as the return window is limited to 15 days.

## Exclusions

The following items are excluded from this return policy:

- Software downloads and digital goods
- Opened consumables such as batteries and printer ink
- Electronics with manufacturer seals broken beyond 15 days
- Products damaged due to misuse or customer negligence

All exclusions are intended to minimize fraud risk and avoid resale of used or damaged goods.

## Refunds

Refunds will be issued in the original form of payment after successful validation of the returned item and confirmation that it meets policy conditions.

- Refund processing time is up to 10 business days after receipt and inspection of the returned product.
- Partial refunds or store credit may apply if products are returned damaged or missing accessories.
- Refunds are not provided for returns made after the 15-day period or for excluded items.

## Policy Statements

- The return window for all electronic products is 15 days from purchase (NRG-POL-CX-004, version 2.0).
- Returns outside the 15-day window will be rejected.
- The policy is approved and effective as of November 1, 2025.
- All customer interactions regarding returns must comply with this policy to maintain consistency.
- Employees are required to verify purchase date and condition before accepting returns.

## Controls and Metrics

To ensure policy adherence:

- Returns are logged and tracked through the Nirvana Retail Group returns management system.
- Return rate metrics on electronics products will be monitored quarterly.
- Any return requests beyond 15 days will be flagged and subject to denial.
- Customer service audits will randomly verify compliance with policy statements.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Customer Experience Representatives:** Verify return eligibility, inspect items, and process refunds per policy.
- **Store Managers:** Approve exceptions within the limits defined in the Exceptions and Escalation section.
- **Quality Control Team:** Inspect returned electronics for condition and report discrepancies.
- **Customer Experience Leadership:** Monitor returns metrics, conduct audits, and update policy as needed.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Exceptions to the 15-day return window are not permitted under normal circumstances.
- In rare cases involving manufacturer defects verified post-return window, escalation to Customer Experience Leadership is required.
- Any exceptions granted must be documented and approved by a manager, with records maintained for audit purposes.

## Review Cycle

This policy shall undergo formal review every 24 months. The next scheduled review will be by November 2027 or sooner if changes in market conditions or regulations necessitate it.
