# California Laptop Promotion Policy

## Executive Summary

This document, identified as **NRG-POL-SALES-001**, outlines the policy framework and operational guidelines for the California Laptop Promotion conducted by Nirvana Retail Group’s Sales department. The policy version **1.0** was effective from **2025-01-01** and expired on **2025-03-31**. The promotion offered a **10%** discount on products classified under the category **COMPUTING-LAPTOPS** exclusively within the **California** region. This policy is now marked as **expired**, and no further application of the discount is authorized under this guideline.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this policy is to establish standardized rules and procedures for applying the California Laptop Promotion throughout all applicable retail locations and online sales channels serving the California region. It specifies eligibility criteria, discount values, promotion period, and applicable exceptions to ensure uniformity and compliance within Nirvana Retail Group operations. The policy scope is limited to the category **COMPUTING-LAPTOPS**.

## Purpose

This policy serves to:

- Drive sales growth and customer engagement for laptop products in California.
- Define eligibility and procedural controls for the promotion.
- Ensure legal and corporate compliance when offering and communicating discounts.

## Eligibility

All retail sales and authorized online sales transactions within the **California** region targeting the product category **COMPUTING-LAPTOPS** were eligible for the promotion during the specified period. Eligibility applied only to products explicitly categorized as laptops within the company inventory systems.

## Promotion Period

The promotion was valid starting **2025-01-01** and concluding on **2025-03-31**. Sales transactions outside this date range were not eligible for the promotional discount.

## Discount

A fixed discount of **10%** was applied on qualifying laptop products under the category **COMPUTING-LAPTOPS**. No other discounts or promotions were combinable with this offer, and the discount was applied to the pre-tax sale price.

## Exceptions

The following exceptions applied during the promotion period:

- Clearance or already-discounted laptop items were excluded from the **10%** discount.
- Corporate or bulk purchase orders did not qualify unless explicitly approved by Sales management.
- Gift card purchases or bundled packages combining laptops with non-computing accessories were excluded.

## Policy Statements

- All Sales personnel were required to apply the **10%** discount only within the set promotion window and region.
- Pricing systems were to be configured to automatically apply the discount on eligible products during the promotion period.
- Unauthorized extension of the promotion beyond **2025-03-31** was prohibited and subject to disciplinary review.
- Communication of the promotion was restricted to authorized marketing channels targeting California customers.

## Controls and Metrics

To ensure adherence and measure promotion success, the following controls and metrics were established:

- Daily sales reports filtered by region and product category to track discount application.
- Audit logs to verify compliance with promotion period and discount rules.
- Exception reporting for any transactions where the discount was incorrectly applied outside of eligibility.
- Post-promotion sales analysis comparing laptop sales volume before, during, and after the campaign period.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Sales Department**: Responsible for enforcing the policy, applying discounts during the promotional period, and reporting compliance.
- **Retail Store Managers**: Ensure staff are trained and adhere strictly to eligibility and discount rules.
- **IT and Pricing Teams**: Implement technical controls in POS systems to support automatic discounting.
- **Compliance and Audit Teams**: Monitor and audit discount application and report any breaches.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Any exceptions to this policy or requests for exceptions must be escalated to the Sales Director for approval. Unauthorized deviations must be reported to the Compliance Manager immediately. Non-compliance may lead to sanctions including retraining, reprimand, or revocation of promotional privileges.

## Review Cycle

This policy version **1.0** was valid from **2025-01-01** to **2025-03-31** and is now **expired**. Policies related to California laptop promotions must be reviewed and updated annually or as determined by business needs and market conditions.
