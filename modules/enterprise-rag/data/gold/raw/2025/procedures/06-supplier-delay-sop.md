# Supplier Delay Standard Operating Procedure (NRG-SOP-SUP-006)

## Executive Summary

This document defines the standard operating procedure (SOP) for managing supplier delays within Nirvana Retail Group’s United States Supply Chain department. Timely identification, communication, and escalation of supplier delays are essential to minimize supply chain disruptions and maintain customer satisfaction. This SOP provides clear triggers, procedural steps, controls, and escalation protocols. It reflects version 1.2 of the SOP and holds an approved status, effective from September 1, 2025.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this SOP is to establish consistent and effective handling of supplier delivery delays. It applies to all Supply Chain personnel responsible for supplier management and logistics within the United States region. The procedure ensures supplier delays are detected early, communicated adequately, and escalated appropriately after a defined time period to mitigate operational risk.

## Triggers

- Delay notification received from a supplier.
- Internal system flags a shipment delay.
- Supplier fails to meet agreed delivery date without prior notice.
- Any indication of potential impact on demand fulfillment timelines.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the supplier management system and delivery tracking software.
- Current contact information for all approved suppliers.
- Familiarity with supply contracts specifying delivery terms.
- Training on internal communication and escalation protocols.

## Procedure Steps

1. **Identify Delay:** Monitor incoming delivery statuses daily. Confirm any supplier delay beyond the agreed delivery schedule.
2. **Document Delay:** Record delay details in the supplier management system, including reason, expected new delivery date, and supplier contact.
3. **Notify Internal Stakeholders:** Alert the Procurement Lead and relevant logistics team via email or system alert immediately after delay recognition.
4. **Communicate with Supplier:** Contact the supplier to obtain detailed delay cause, recovery plan, and revised delivery timelines.
5. **Update Records:** Maintain all delay communication and responses within the system for audit and reporting purposes.

## Controls and Metrics

- **Delay Reporting Accuracy:** 100% of identified delays must be logged within 2 business hours.
- **Escalation Compliance:** Delays exceeding established thresholds must be escalated within 24 hours.
- **Supplier Performance Tracking:** Monthly reports generated to analyze delay patterns and supplier reliability.
- **Communication Audits:** Regular review of internal and supplier communications to ensure procedural adherence.

## Escalation

If a supplier delay persists beyond **24** hours without resolution or clear recovery plan, the issue must be escalated as follows:

1. Notify the Supply Chain Director immediately.
2. Engage cross-functional teams including Procurement, Operations, and Customer Service for impact assessment.
3. Initiate contingency planning such as alternative sourcing or expedited shipping.
4. Document escalation actions and outcomes in the supplier management system.

Escalation helps minimize risks associated with prolonged supplier delays and ensures executive visibility for critical disruptions.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Supply Chain Team:** Identifies, documents, and communicates delays; initiates escalation when required.
- **Procurement Lead:** Liaises with suppliers, manages supplier relationships, and supports resolution plans.
- **Logistics Coordinator:** Assesses delivery schedules and adjusts internal logistics plans accordingly.
- **Supply Chain Director:** Receives escalated issues and coordinates higher-level response.
- **Quality Assurance:** Reviews reported delays for trends affecting product quality or compliance.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Minor delays under 24 hours that do not impact demand fulfillment may be managed locally without escalation.
- Force majeure events where suppliers cannot meet delivery timelines will require documented exception approval by the Supply Chain Director.
- Any escalation must be fully documented including justification and corrective actions taken.

## Review Cycle

This SOP will be reviewed annually or as required based on:

- Changes in supplier landscape or contractual terms.
- Audit findings related to delay management.
- Updates to regional compliance or operational requirements.
- Feedback from stakeholders on SOP effectiveness.
