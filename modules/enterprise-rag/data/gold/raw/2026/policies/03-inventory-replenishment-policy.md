# Inventory Replenishment Policy

## Executive Summary

This Inventory Replenishment Policy (document_id: NRG-POL-INV-003, version 3.1) establishes standardized procedures and controls to maintain optimal inventory levels across the United States supply chain operations at Nirvana Retail Group. The policy ensures demand fulfillment and mitigates stockouts by defining reorder points, escalation paths, and performance metrics. Key references include the standard reorder point of 100 sellable units and example SKU NRG-LAP-1001 for laptops. This document is approved and effective as of January 15, 2026.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this policy is to define the inventory replenishment framework and controls for all sellable goods managed by the Supply Chain department within the United States region. The scope encompasses all physical inventory items stored at warehouses and retail locations, with special emphasis on high-demand categories such as laptops. The policy governs reorder thresholds, approval authorities, and escalation procedures to maintain service levels and reduce excess carrying costs.

## Purpose

To maintain continuous product availability and balance inventory investment with customer demand by implementing standardized reorder points, metrics, and escalation protocols.

## Reorder Rules

- Inventory must be monitored continuously.
- When stock levels of any SKU reach the predetermined reorder point, replenishment orders must be triggered.
- The standard reorder point for laptops is set at **100 sellable units**.
- For example, laptop SKU **NRG-LAP-1001** serves as a referenced case for reorder management.
- Replenishment orders must be calibrated to meet forecasted demand without exceeding storage or budgetary constraints.

## Escalation

- If inventory approaches or falls below reorder points without confirmed replenishment orders within 48 business hours, the Supply Chain Manager must be notified.
- In cases where escalated inventory risks remain unresolved after 72 business hours, the escalation will advance to the Director of Supply Chain Operations.
- Unusual supply disruptions or forecast inaccuracies require immediate reporting to the Supply Chain Executive Team.

## Metrics

- Compliance with reorder point triggers (target: 100% adherence).
- Average time between reorder point hit and order confirmation.
- Stockout incidence rate by SKU category.
- Inventory turnover ratio measured quarterly.
- Performance benchmark tied to the standard reorder point of **100 sellable units** for laptops.

## Policy Statements

- All product inventory must be replenished proactively based on established reorder points.
- The SKU **NRG-LAP-1001** exemplifies standard reorder management practices and must meet the reorder point threshold consistently.
- Out-of-stock situations must be minimized through adherence to reorder lead times.
- Version **3.1** of this policy supersedes all prior versions and reflects updated operational metrics and escalation workflows.

## Controls and Metrics

- Automated inventory tracking systems shall enforce reorder alerts at the **100 sellable units** threshold.
- Replenishment orders require validation by the Supply Chain Planning team before procurement execution.
- Periodic audits will assess compliance with reorder rules and escalation procedures.
- KPI dashboards shall incorporate the defined metrics for ongoing performance monitoring.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Inventory Analysts:** Monitor stock levels continuously and initiate reorder triggers at the **100 sellable units** point.
- **Supply Chain Manager:** Review escalated cases and ensure timely replenishment actions.
- **Director of Supply Chain Operations:** Oversee unresolved escalations and adjust policy implementation as necessary.
- **Supply Chain Executive Team:** Provide governance on supply disruption responses and approve exception handling.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Exceptions to reorder points may be approved for seasonal or promotional items upon documented forecast revisions.
- Emergency escalations outside normal hours require direct communication with Supply Chain leadership.
- Persistent failure to meet reorder criteria will prompt review and may initiate corrective action plans.
