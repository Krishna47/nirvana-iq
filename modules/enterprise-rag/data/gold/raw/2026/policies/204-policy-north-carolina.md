# Customer Experience Policy — North Carolina #204

## Executive Summary  
This policy outlines Nirvana Retail Group’s commitments and procedural standards for delivering an exceptional customer experience within the **North Carolina** region. Focusing on the **Nirvana One Phone (product code NRG-MOB-3001)**, the guideline mandates operational consistency, measurement controls, and escalation processes to maintain high satisfaction levels. Implementing a sample size of **84** units during **week number 49** each year, this framework supports actionable insights guiding customer care enhancements. This document, identified as **NRG-POL-GOLD-0204**, is approved and effective from **January 16, 2026**, under the jurisdiction of the **Customer Experience** department.

## Purpose and Scope  
The purpose of this policy is to establish clear expectations and standardized procedures for managing customer interactions and feedback specifically related to the Nirvana One Phone (**NRG-MOB-3001**) within the **North Carolina** region. This policy applies to all Customer Experience team members, retail staff, and third-party service providers engaged in customer service activities covering this product line in the defined region.

## Summary  
This policy defines customer experience obligations focusing on responsiveness, product knowledge, and issue resolution for the Nirvana One Phone. Ensuring the delivery of exceptional service will be governed by pre-set metrics and controls tested on a sample size of **84 units** during **week number 49** annually.

## Policy Statements  
1. All personnel must provide timely, accurate, and courteous customer service for Nirvana One Phone inquiries and issues.  
2. Feedback must be collected using standardized formats on the specified sample size of **84 units** during **week number 49** each year.  
3. Any deviation from service standards must be documented and escalated per the Exceptions and Escalation section.  
4. Continuous training on product updates and customer interaction best practices is mandatory for staff handling Nirvana One Phone support.  

## Controls and Metrics  
- Customer feedback and service performance are measured annually with a sample of **84 units** during **week number 49** to ensure compliance with quality standards.  
- Metrics include resolution time, satisfaction scores, and first-contact resolution rates specific to the Nirvana One Phone (**NRG-MOB-3001**).  
- Non-compliance trends are tracked monthly and reviewed by the Customer Experience leadership team.  

## Roles and Responsibilities  
- **Customer Experience Department:** Implement and monitor compliance with this policy, conduct training, analyze metrics, and report performance in the North Carolina region.  
- **Customer Service Representatives:** Follow procedures, gather customer feedback on the Nirvana One Phone, and document any service gaps.  
- **Regional Managers:** Ensure adherence to policy directives and manage escalation processes within their teams.  

## Exceptions and Escalation  
- Exceptions to this policy are limited to unavoidable operational disruptions or product-specific issues beyond team control.  
- All exceptions must be reported immediately to the Customer Experience Department head with justification and identified corrective actions.  
- Escalation procedures include prompt notification of the Customer Experience Quality Assurance lead and involvement of regional management for resolution within 48 hours.  

## Review Cycle  
- This policy will be reviewed annually, no later than one calendar year from the effective date, or sooner if operational changes affect customer experience.  
- The review includes assessment of customer feedback performance during **week number 49** for the **Nirvana One Phone (NRG-MOB-3001)**.
