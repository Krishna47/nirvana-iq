# Store Operations Policy — Arizona #348

## Executive Summary

This policy document, NRG-POL-GOLD-0348, establishes the operational standards and procedures for the Store Operations department within the Arizona region. It specifically addresses handling, display, and management protocols related to our key product, the Nirvana One Phone (product code NRG-MOB-3001). This policy applies from the effective date of January 25, 2026, and is a critical framework designed to optimize store performance and customer satisfaction.

---

## Summary

NRG-POL-GOLD-0348 defines store operations procedures tailored to Arizona stores, focusing on maintaining quality control and operational consistency in handling the Nirvana One Phone (NRG-MOB-3001). The policy includes metrics derived from a sample size of 108 units during week number 37 and sets forth controls, roles, and exception handling measures relevant to these locations.

---

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that all stores in the Arizona region execute consistent and high-quality operations relating to the selling and management of the Nirvana One Phone (product code NRG-MOB-3001). This includes inventory handling, customer service standards, and compliance with Nirvana Retail Group’s corporate policies. This policy is mandatory for all Store Operations staff within the Arizona region.

---

## Policy Statements

- All stores must adhere strictly to the handling and operational practices for the Nirvana One Phone (NRG-MOB-3001) as defined in this policy.
- The minimum sample size for quality assessments is set at 108 units, based on week number 37 operational data.
- Any operational changes impacting product handling or store processes must be communicated promptly to regional management.
- Records of product handling and customer interactions must be maintained in accordance with corporate data policies.

---

## Details

This policy governs the following operational aspects for Arizona stores:

- Receiving and inspection protocols for incoming shipments of Nirvana One Phone units.
- Display and merchandising guidelines to optimize visibility and customer engagement.
- Inventory control checks at regular intervals, supported by statistical sampling (108 units per week 37 benchmark).
- Reporting procedures for any product anomalies or operational deviations.

## Controls

- Random sampling of 108 Nirvana One Phone units per week (week number 37 standard) to monitor quality and compliance.
- Mandatory audits conducted quarterly with documented results submitted to regional and department heads.
- Authorization levels for operational adjustments defined to maintain control integrity.
