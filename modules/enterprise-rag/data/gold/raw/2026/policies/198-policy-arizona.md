## Summary

The Procurement Policy detailed in **NRG-POL-GOLD-0198** applies to all procurement activities related to the Nirvana Air 14 (product code **NRG-LAP-1001**) within the Arizona region. The policy establishes a framework for supplier selection, sample inspection, compliance controls, and escalation processes with the objective of maintaining procurement excellence and mitigating supply chain risks.

## Purpose and Scope

This policy provides standardized procedures and guidelines for procuring the Nirvana Air 14 (**NRG-LAP-1001**) in Arizona. It applies to all employees, contractors, and partner vendors engaged in procurement activities in this territory. The primary goal is to ensure that procurement decisions are consistent, compliant with corporate regulations, and aligned with business objectives.

## Policy Statements

- All procurement transactions for the Nirvana Air 14 (**NRG-LAP-1001**) in Arizona must conform to the procedures set forth in this document.
- A minimum of **118** sample units shall be inspected during procurement week number **43** to verify quality standards before contract finalization.
- Suppliers must meet Nirvana Retail Group’s quality benchmarks and delivery timelines stipulated for the Arizona region.
- Any deviation from approved procurement processes requires documented justification and formal approval from the Procurement department leadership.

---

## Details

Procurement operations shall:
- Utilize qualified and vetted suppliers capable of supplying the Nirvana Air 14 (**NRG-LAP-1001**) consistent with the company’s standards.
- Adhere to the timing requirements, especially focusing on procurement activities aligned with week number **43** for quality assurance sample collection.
- Document all procurement workflows, supplier agreements, and sample testing outcomes for audit readiness.

---

## Controls and Metrics

- Inspection of **118** sample units will be mandatory each procurement cycle to assess seller compliance and product integrity.
- Procurement transactions will be audited biannually to verify adherence to this policy.
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) include supplier delivery adherence rates, sample acceptance rates, and cost variance reports.
- Weekly procurement data reports should highlight findings from week number **43** inspections for managerial review.

---

## Controls

- Suppliers must provide full traceability for the Nirvana Air 14 (**NRG-LAP-1001**).
- Procurement staff will maintain records of all sample units tested and outcomes during week number **43** every fiscal year.
- Automated procurement system checks will flag deviations or anomalies requiring escalation.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Procurement Managers** are responsible for enforcing this policy within the Arizona region.
- **Quality Assurance Teams** must conduct the sample inspection of **118** units during week number **43** and report results.
- **Supplier Relationship Coordinators** will oversee supplier performance and compliance with this policy.
- All stakeholders must escalate exceptions promptly as outlined in the Exceptions and Escalation section.

---

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Exceptions to this policy require documented approval from the Procurement Director for Arizona.
- Escalations must be initiated immediately when sample inspections indicate product deviations or supply chain disruptions.
- Failure to comply with sampling or procurement controls may result in supplier suspension or contract review.
