This policy, document ID NRG-POL-SALES-001, version 2.0, outlines the parameters for the 12% discount applied exclusively to the COMPUTING-LAPTOPS category within the California region. The promotion is effective from 2026-01-01 and ends on 2026-03-31. The status of this policy is approved, authorizing all Sales team members in California to implement the specified discount within the defined period. Controls and roles are established to ensure compliance and effective monitoring throughout the promotional lifecycle.

## Purpose and Scope
The purpose of this policy is to formally define the terms and conditions of the laptop promotion in California, ensuring alignment between Sales operations and company strategic objectives. This policy applies exclusively to the Sales department in the California region, covering all transactions involving products categorized under COMPUTING-LAPTOPS.

## Purpose
To increase laptop sales and market penetration in California through a time-limited discount while maintaining pricing integrity and operational controls.

## Eligibility
All sales channels and sales personnel operating within the California region are eligible to apply the promotional discount to eligible products classified under category COMPUTING-LAPTOPS.

## Promotion Period
The laptop promotion is valid from 2026-01-01 until 2026-03-31. All discounts must be applied strictly within this timeframe. Any transaction dated outside this period is not eligible for the 12% discount.

## Discount
A discount of 12% applies to all products in the COMPUTING-LAPTOPS category for eligible customers within California during the Promotion Period.

## Exceptions
- Products not within the COMPUTING-LAPTOPS category are excluded from this promotion.
- Customers must not combine this promotion with any other offers or discounts unless explicitly authorized by Sales Management.
- Transactions occurring outside the effective date range (2026-01-01 to 2026-03-31) are ineligible for the discount.

## Policy Statements
- The 12% discount is the authorized promotional rate for all laptops sold in California during the promotion period.
- Sales personnel must adhere to the region-specific terms outlined in this policy.
- Unauthorized discounting beyond 12% or outside the specified period may result in disciplinary actions.
- The promotion supports the strategic sales goals of increasing laptop market share in California within Q1 2026.
