This policy, identified by document ID **NRG-POL-GOLD-0444**, establishes the merchandising standards and guidelines specifically for the **North Carolina** region within the **Merchandising** department. It governs the presentation and handling of the product **Nirvana Gamer X** (product code **NRG-LAP-1004**) during key sales periods, including sample unit requirements and weekly operational benchmarks. The objective is to ensure consistent product availability, visual merchandising compliance, and uphold Nirvana Retail Group’s brand standards throughout this region. This policy is effective from January 13, 2026, and approved under version **1.0**.

## Summary  
The policy sets clear rules for the merchandising of the Nirvana Gamer X (NRG-LAP-1004) products in the North Carolina stores. It includes mandatory usage of **84 sample units** per location for **week number 29**, ensuring adequate product visibility and customer interaction opportunities. Compliance with these standards directly impacts sales performance and customer experience.

## Purpose and Scope  
The purpose of NRG-POL-GOLD-0444 is to standardize merchandising practices within the North Carolina region to:

- Enhance customer engagement with the Nirvana Gamer X product.
- Maintain uniform visual merchandising standards.
- Facilitate operational consistency across all regional outlets.

This policy applies exclusively to the Merchandising department’s handling of the Nirvana Gamer X (product code NRG-LAP-1004) within the North Carolina region.

## Policy Statements  
1. All North Carolina merchandising locations must maintain at least **84 sample units** of Nirvana Gamer X (NRG-LAP-1004) on the sales floor during **week number 29**.  
2. Sample units must be displayed according to the approved visual merchandising layout guidelines published by the Merchandising department.  
3. Product placements must prioritize high-traffic areas to maximize consumer interaction with NRG-LAP-1004.  
4. All staff involved in merchandising must complete training on product knowledge and display standards before the start of the merchandising week.  
5. Any deviation from the minimum sample unit requirements must be formally approved through the Exceptions and Escalation process.

Requests must be submitted in writing to the Merchandising Manager no later than two weeks prior to week number 29. The Merchandising Manager will review and approve or deny exceptions with documented rationale. Appeals follow the standard escalation path to the Regional Merchandising Director.
