# Information Security Policy

## Executive Summary

This Information Security Policy (version 4.0) establishes the framework to protect Nirvana Retail Group’s information assets in the United States region. Approved and effective as of January 1, 2026, this policy mandates security controls that safeguard data confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Among its key directives is the requirement for multi-factor authentication (MFA) to mitigate unauthorized access risks. The document outlines access control mechanisms, incident reporting procedures, roles and responsibilities, and compliance metrics. This policy remains in approved status to reflect current best practices and regulatory requirements.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this policy is to define the requirements and standards for information security within Nirvana Retail Group. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing company information systems in the United States. This policy covers all information assets, including electronic data, physical documents, and communication channels, ensuring consistent protection from internal and external threats.

## Purpose

To ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Nirvana Retail Group’s information assets by defining security requirements and controls aligned with industry standards and regulatory mandates.

## Access Control

Access to information systems and data shall be granted based on business needs and the principle of least privilege. All access rights must be reviewed periodically to prevent unauthorized usage. Access controls shall include unique user identification, role-based permissions, and logging of access activities.

## MFA

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for access to all Nirvana Retail Group information systems containing sensitive or critical data. The policy mandates MFA to reduce the risk of unauthorized access due to compromised credentials. 

**MFA Required:** true

This requirement applies to remote access, privileged accounts, and any system interfacing with customer or financial data.

## Incident Reporting

All security incidents, including suspected breaches, unauthorized access, or data loss, must be reported immediately to the Information Security department through established channels. Prompt reporting enables rapid response, containment, and mitigation to minimize impact.

## Policy Statements

- The information security policy is in **approved** status and applicable from January 1, 2026.
- Version 4.0 represents the current and authoritative document.
- MFA is mandatory across all relevant systems.
- Access control procedures must enforce least privilege and periodic review.
- Security training on policy adherence is required for all personnel.
- Incident reporting protocols must be followed without exception.

## Controls and Metrics

To ensure compliance with this policy, the following controls will be monitored:

- **MFA Enforcement:** Percentage of users enabled with MFA should be 100%.
- **Access Reviews:** Quarterly audits to verify appropriate access levels.
- **Incident Response Time:** Average time to acknowledge and respond to security incidents.
- **Training Completion:** Percentage of employees completing annual security awareness training.

Metric results will be reported to the Information Security governance committee every quarter.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Information Security Department:** Develops, implements, and maintains this policy; monitors compliance; and leads incident response.
- **Managers:** Ensure team members comply with access control and MFA requirements.
- **All Employees:** Adhere to security controls, promptly report security incidents, and complete required training.
- **IT Operations:** Implement technical controls, enforce MFA, and support system access audits.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this policy require documented approval from the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). Any granted exceptions must include a defined risk mitigation plan and a limited timeframe. Unapproved deviations from policy will be escalated to senior leadership and may result in disciplinary action.

## Review Cycle

This policy is subject to an annual review or more frequently if required by regulatory changes or security incidents. The next scheduled review will be on January 1, 2027.
