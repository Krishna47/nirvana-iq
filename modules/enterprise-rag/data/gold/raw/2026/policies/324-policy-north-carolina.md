The purpose of this policy is to establish clear, consistent procurement procedures for the North Carolina region, minimizing risks associated with supply chain interruptions, quality failures, and contractual non-compliance.

- All purchase orders for product code **NRG-LAP-1001**.
- Quality sample testing, including weekly sample reviews; a primary example is from **week_number 13** where **84 sample_units** were examined.
- Vendor management, including onboarding, performance monitoring, and compliance.

## Policy Statements

- All purchases of **NRG-LAP-1001** must be authorized through the Procurement department following the outlined procedures.
- Quality assurance must include weekly inspections, sampling at a minimum of 84 units per batch, reflecting the sample size used in **week_number 13**.
- Suppliers must meet predetermined criteria on delivery timelines, quality standards, and cost metrics.
- Any deviation from procurement standards must be documented and escalated according to the Exceptions and Escalation section.
- Procurement staff must maintain accurate records of all transactions and inspections related to **NRG-LAP-1001**.

## Controls

To ensure compliance with this policy:

- Procurement activities will be audited quarterly by internal review teams.

- Escalation procedures apply to unresolved quality or supply issues.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Procurement Managers:** Authorize and oversee purchasing activities, ensure supplier adherence, and enforce sampling standards.
- **Quality Assurance Team:** Conduct product inspections, confirm sample unit integrity, and report findings.
- **Vendor Management:** Maintain approved supplier lists, manage supplier performance reviews.
- **Regional Compliance Officer:** Ensure all procurement activities comply with North Carolina regional regulatory requirements.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this policy require documented justification and approval from the Procurement Director. Situations warranting escalation include:

- Sample unit failures exceeding 5% in any weekly batch inspection.
- Delivery delays impacting operational schedules.
- Non-compliance with product code **NRG-LAP-1001** specifications.

Escalations should be addressed initially by the Procurement Manager; unresolved issues progress to the Regional Compliance Officer.
