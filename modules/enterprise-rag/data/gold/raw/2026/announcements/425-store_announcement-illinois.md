# Information Security Store Announcement — Illinois #425

## Executive Summary

This document, NRG-ANN-GOLD-0425, issued by the Information Security department, announces a critical information security review and control update concerning the Nirvana Flex 13 product, product code NRG-LAP-1003, currently in distribution across Illinois stores. The announcement addresses findings from sample audits involving 105 sample units during week number 10. All stores operating within the Illinois region are required to comply with the outlined security controls and procedures effective June 21, 2026. This announcement is approved and supersedes any previous guidance relating to this product.

## Summary

The Information Security department is implementing enhanced security measures for the Nirvana Flex 13 (product code NRG-LAP-1003) across all Illinois stores. This action is part of the ongoing commitment to safeguarding product integrity and customer data. The included sample of 105 units has undergone rigorous evaluation in week number 10, yielding actionable insights that have driven these updates.

The audit identified multiple risk vectors that could impact product security if not adequately managed throughout the store-level handling and customer engagement lifecycle. Illinois-region store managers must ensure all staff are trained and compliant with security protocols detailed below.

## Controls

To mitigate identified risks, the following controls are mandatory for all Illinois stores handling the Nirvana Flex 13:

1. **Access Control:** Only authorized personnel with validated security clearance may access or unpack the NRG-LAP-1003 units.  
2. **Product Verification:** Each unit from the batch of 105 sampled must be inspected for tampering or signs of compromise.  
3. **Data Protection:** Customer data handling procedures associated with Nirvana Flex 13 sales must comply with current corporate information security policies.  
4. **Incident Reporting:** Any discrepancies or suspected breaches during inspection or sales must be reported immediately via the Information Security incident reporting system.  
5. **Training:** All sales floor and inventory staff must complete the Illinois regional security compliance training before handling the product.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Store Managers:** Enforce adherence to security controls, oversee training completion, and report incidents.  
- **Inventory Teams:** Perform thorough verification and physical inspection of all Nirvana Flex 13 units received.  
- **Information Security Department:** Provide guidance, update training content, and review incident reports.  
- **Regional Security Officers:** Monitor compliance audits and escalate significant issues as needed.  

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to the defined controls may only be authorized by the Information Security department. Any suspected security incident or non-compliance must be escalated immediately to the Regional Security Officer and Information Security department for investigation and resolution. Delayed reporting may increase the risk to product and customer safety, potentially resulting in disciplinary measures.
