## Executive Summary  
This announcement outlines the marketing initiative for the Nirvana Flex 13, product code **NRG-LAP-1003**, to be rolled out across the **Texas** region starting week number **12**. A total of **91** sample units will be distributed to select stores for promotional and demo purposes. The plan, developed by the **Marketing** department, has been reviewed and is **approved** as version **1.0**. This document provides all necessary background, controls, and points of contact to ensure smooth execution.

## Summary  
The focus of this store announcement is the introduction and promotional campaign of the Nirvana Flex 13 laptop in Texas stores during week number **12** of the operational calendar. The marketing campaign will utilize **91** sample units strategically allocated across retail locations to maximize customer engagement and sales conversion.

Sample units will be distributed to store locations within the Texas region for customer demonstrations and promotional events. Training material and marketing collateral specific to the Nirvana Flex 13 will accompany the sample devices to ensure consistent messaging.

## Controls  
- Sample units must be tracked via serial numbers and logged in the inventory management system before and after the promotional period.  
- Only authorized personnel from the Marketing department and store managers may handle the sample units.  
- The deployment schedule must adhere strictly to week number 12 to maintain campaign alignment.  
- Stores must report usage metrics, customer feedback, and unit condition weekly to the Marketing regional coordinator.  
- Compliance with all company retail presentation standards and health and safety guidelines is mandatory during customer demonstrations.

## Roles and Responsibilities  
- **Marketing Department:** Overall campaign planning, distribution of sample units, creation and delivery of promotional materials, performance tracking.  
- **Store Managers (Texas region):** Receiving sample units, facilitating customer demonstrations, reporting feedback, and coordinating with Marketing.  
- **Inventory Control Team:** Logging receipt and return of sample units ensuring asset security throughout the campaign.  
- **Regional Marketing Coordinator:** Oversees campaign execution across Texas stores, consolidates reports, and escalates issues as needed.

## Exceptions and Escalation  
- Any deviations from the scheduled distribution in week number 12 must be approved in writing by the Marketing department head.  
- Missing or damaged sample units must be reported immediately to the Inventory Control Team and the Regional Marketing Coordinator.  
- Should performance metrics fall below expected thresholds, the Regional Marketing Coordinator must escalate to Marketing leadership within 48 hours for intervention.  
- Customer complaints related to product demonstrations must be recorded and addressed per company customer service protocols.
