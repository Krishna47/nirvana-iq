# Information Security Store Announcement — Georgia #257

## Executive Summary

This document NRG-ANN-GOLD-0257, issued by the Information Security department, announces a focused information security review and monitoring activity for the product **Nirvana Air Purifier** (product code **NRG-HOM-2002**) across the **Georgia** region. During week number **50**, a total of **97** sample units will be randomly selected and subjected to enhanced security checks to mitigate risks associated with supply chain vulnerabilities and device integrity. This announcement is effective from **June 15, 2026**, and is approved as version **1.0** for immediate implementation.

## Summary

In alignment with Nirvana Retail Group’s commitment to maintaining the highest standards of information security, this announcement pertains to implementing targeted security verification procedures on **Nirvana Air Purifier** units distributed in the **Georgia** region. The initiative involves examining **97** sample units to ensure compliance with corporate security policies and to identify any potential security threats before wider distribution.

This measure supports proactive risk management in product deployments that incorporate connected technology. It further aligns with our ongoing efforts to safeguard Nirvana's brand and customer trust through operational vigilance directed by the Information Security department.

The selected units will undergo a comprehensive security inspection focusing on firmware integrity, network interface security, and compliance with encryption standards. Any anomalies discovered will prompt immediate action per escalation protocols detailed below.

## Controls

- **Sampling Procedure:** Random selection of 97 units will be conducted across all Georgia stores to ensure statistically relevant coverage.  
- **Firmware Verification:** Validation of software signatures against approved version records.  
- **Network Security Checks:** Assessment of wireless configurations for vulnerabilities.  
- **Data Protection Compliance:** Confirmation that device settings meet Nirvana Retail Group data privacy and cybersecurity policies.  
- **Reporting:** Documented results to be submitted within one week post-inspection to the Information Security department for review.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Store Managers (Georgia Region):** Facilitate unit selection and provide access to devices for inspection.  
- **Information Security Analysts:** Execute the security checks, document findings, and recommend corrective measures.  
- **Regional IT Support:** Support firmware validation and network configuration assessments.  
- **Compliance Officer:** Monitor adherence to corporate and regulatory security standards during the process.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to the sampling or inspection process may only be approved by the Information Security Director under documented circumstances such as supply chain disruptions or technological incompatibilities. All irregularities or security incidents found during inspections must be escalated immediately to the Information Security Incident Response Team via the corporate hotline or internal ticketing system.

## Review Cycle

This announcement and the attached procedures will undergo a formal review every 12 months or earlier if triggered by significant security incidents, changes in technology, or updates in regulatory standards. The next review date is scheduled for **June 15, 2027**.
