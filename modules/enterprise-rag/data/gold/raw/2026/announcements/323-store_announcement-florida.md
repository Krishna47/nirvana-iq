## Controls

- Stores must follow established Supply Chain receiving procedures, including documentation and system entry tied to this announcement ID **NRG-ANN-GOLD-0323**.  
- Any damaged or missing units must be logged and escalated per the Exceptions and Escalation section.  
- Inventory systems should be updated within 24 hours of receipt to reflect the new stock and ongoing monitoring.
