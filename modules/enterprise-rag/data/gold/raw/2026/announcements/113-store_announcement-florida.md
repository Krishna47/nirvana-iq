# Supply Chain Store Announcement — Florida #113

## Executive Summary
This announcement pertains to the deployment and sample distribution of the **Nirvana Air Purifier** identified by product code **NRG-HOM-2002** in the Florida region. A sample quantity of **113** units will be made available during **week number 10** to support sales testing and customer feedback collection. The document is issued by the **Supply Chain** department and is effective starting **June 6, 2026**. This version is **1.0** and has been **approved** for immediate implementation across designated Florida stores.

## Summary
The Supply Chain department is executing a targeted sample distribution of the Nirvana Air Purifier (**NRG-HOM-2002**) in select Florida retail locations. A total of **113** sample units will be allocated in week 10 of the fiscal year to enhance customer experience and gather real-time insights on product performance. This initiative is part of strategic efforts to improve product visibility and sales conversion in the Florida market.

This deployment seeks to expedite the presence of Nirvana Air Purifiers in high-traffic Florida stores, building customer trust through hands-on product interaction. The sample units will be displayed strategically in stores to maximize visibility and availability for customer demos.

## Controls
To ensure proper distribution and monitoring of sample units, the following controls will be in place:  
- Inventory tracking of each of the 113 sample units deployed.  
- Weekly reporting on sample interaction metrics including customer engagement and feedback collated by store staff.  
- Store managers must confirm receipt and placement of sample units by the Monday of week 10.  
- Samples are not for sale and must be safeguarded against unauthorized removal or damage.  
- Any discrepancies in sample unit counts must be reported to Supply Chain immediately.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Supply Chain Department:** Oversee the dispatch, tracking, and reporting of sample units in Florida stores.  
- **Store Managers (Florida #113):** Ensure proper receipt, security, and display of sample units. Provide timely feedback through established reporting channels.  
- **Regional Supply Chain Lead:** Monitor adherence to controls and escalate any issues related to the sample deployment.  
- **Sales Associates:** Encourage customer interaction with the air purifier sample and collect applicable feedback for continuous improvement.
