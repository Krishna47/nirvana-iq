# Cloud Operations Store Announcement — Colorado #329

## Executive Summary

This announcement outlines the planned deployment and monitoring of the Nirvana Air Purifier (product code: NRG-HOM-2002) for stores in the Colorado region during week number 18. A total of 89 sample units will be distributed under the management of the Cloud Operations department. The initiative is documented here as part of version 1.0 of the announcement (document ID: NRG-ANN-GOLD-0329), approved and effective as of June 6, 2026.

## Summary

The Nirvana Air Purifier (NRG-HOM-2002) is being introduced across selected Colorado retail outlets to test market adoption and operational effectiveness. This pilot distribution consists of 89 sample units scheduled for release in the 18th calendar week of 2026. This phased approach allows for direct feedback and performance metrics collection to inform larger scale rollouts.

## Details

- **Product Name:** Nirvana Air Purifier  
- **Product Code:** NRG-HOM-2002  
- **Region:** Colorado

- **Week Number of Deployment:** 18  
- **Announcement Version:** 1.0

The deployment will focus on select store locations within the Colorado region. Units will be monitored for customer interactions, reliability under operational conditions, and alignment with environmental safety standards. The Cloud Operations team will coordinate logistics, installation, and data gathering throughout the pilot phase.

## Controls

To ensure integrity and consistency of deployment:

- All sample units must be logged upon receipt and distribution.
- Operational parameters shall be monitored and recorded daily.
- Any unit demonstrating deviation from quality or performance standards will be flagged for immediate review.
- Feedback from each location will be consolidated weekly and reported directly to Cloud Operations leadership.
- Access to sample units and related data is limited to authorized personnel within the Cloud Operations department.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Cloud Operations Department:** Owns the overall planning, logistics, and data collection responsibilities for the pilot introduction of NRG-HOM-2002.
- **Store Managers (Colorado Region):** Oversee on-site handling, customer engagement, and initial troubleshooting of sample units.
- **Quality Assurance Team:** Conducts compliance audits on unit condition and performance.
- **Data Analysts:** Process feedback and operational metrics to evaluate pilot success.
- **Escalation Point of Contact:** Identify and escalate issues beyond store-level resolution to department leads.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to the standard deployment protocol must be documented and approved by the Cloud Operations leadership. Examples include:

- Deferral of unit installation due to store readiness.
- Reported malfunctions requiring immediate unit swap or withdraw.
- Customer safety incidents related to unit operation.

Escalations follow this sequence:

1. Store Manager notifies Cloud Operations lead.
2. Cloud Operations lead evaluates and either resolves or escalates to Quality Assurance or Executive Management.
3. All escalations and resolutions recorded in the incident log for post-pilot review.

## Review Cycle

This announcement and its implementation progress will be reviewed bi-weekly throughout the rollout period. After the pilot completion, a comprehensive review will be scheduled within four weeks to evaluate outcomes and recommend next steps. Document versioning will reflect changes post-review as needed.
