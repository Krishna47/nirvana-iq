# Compliance Store Announcement — Colorado #419

## Executive Summary
This announcement applies to the Colorado region and pertains to the deployment and compliance monitoring of the Nirvana Air Purifier, product code **NRG-HOM-2002**. Effective the week number **4** of the compliance cycle, a total of **99** sample units of this product will be subject to enhanced oversight in all retail stores across the region. This ensures adherence to Nirvana Retail Group’s quality and safety standards as mandated by the Compliance department.

## Summary
The Compliance department issues this store announcement to initiate store-level awareness and actions regarding the **Nirvana Air Purifier (NRG-HOM-2002)** in Colorado stores. This is part of the continuing commitment to uphold product performance standards and consumer safety. Compliance teams and store managers must prepare for increased sampling and documentation efforts starting the week number **4**.

## Details
- **Product Name:** Nirvana Air Purifier  
- **Product Code:** NRG-HOM-2002  
- **Region:** Colorado  
- **Sample Units to Monitor:** 99  
- **Week Number for Deployment:** 4  
- **Effective Date:** June 15, 2026  

All Colorado stores stocking the Nirvana Air Purifier will receive instructions and resources to support the sampling process. This sample size is selected to ensure statistically valid quality control and compliance verification. Stores must maintain detailed records of the sample units tested and any deviations observed.

## Controls
To maintain compliance integrity, the following controls will be enforced:
- Sampling of **99** units per store in Colorado during week number **4** must be completed with traceable documentation.
- All sample testing shall follow established protocols issued by the Compliance department.
- Any unit failing compliance parameters must be reported immediately using the standard escalation procedures.
- Store staff must be trained on proper handling and documentation processes before the effective date to ensure readiness.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Compliance Department:** Oversees policy enforcement, reviews sample data, provides training and support.
- **Store Managers:** Ensure compliance with sampling procedures, maintain documentation, and escalate issues as required.
- **Store Staff:** Execute sampling and testing as instructed, accurately record findings and report discrepancies.
- **Regional Compliance Coordinator (Colorado):** Acts as liaison between corporate Compliance and stores for issue resolution and audit preparation.

## Exceptions and Escalation
- Exceptions to sampling size or process must be pre-approved by the Compliance Department.
- Any deviations identified during sampling must be escalated to the Regional Compliance Coordinator within 24 hours.
- Significant noncompliance findings will trigger immediate investigation and possible store audit.
- Failure to comply with this announcement may lead to disciplinary action consistent with Nirvana Retail Group policies.

## Review Cycle
This document and the associated compliance monitoring process will be reviewed quarterly or upon significant regulatory updates. Feedback from store operations and compliance audits will inform any revisions.
