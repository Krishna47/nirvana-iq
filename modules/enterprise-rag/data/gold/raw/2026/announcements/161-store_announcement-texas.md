# Cloud Operations Store Announcement — Texas #161

## Executive Summary

This announcement details the rollout and operations related to the Nirvana Dock Pro (product code: NRG-ACC-2001) within the Texas region. Focused on week number 6, this communication delivers guidance for the handling and sampling process based on 81 sample units. This document provides critical operational controls, roles, and escalation paths to ensure a smooth deployment and ongoing management of the product across Texas stores under Cloud Operations jurisdiction.

## Summary

The department of Cloud Operations is initiating the controlled distribution of the Nirvana Dock Pro (NRG-ACC-2001) in Texas starting the week number 6. A sample set of 81 units has been allocated as part of this initial phase to evaluate operational readiness and to support store-level integration. This announcement formalizes the procedures, controls, and contacts associated with this deployment.

## Controls

1. **Sample Unit Tracking**  
   All 81 sample units must be logged into the inventory management system with product code NRG-ACC-2001 and tagged for Texas region use only. This enables traceability throughout the deployment period.

2. **Installation Verification**  
   Each unit installed in Texas stores must receive a verification stamp in the system by Cloud Operations staff within 48 hours of receipt.

3. **Operational Reporting**  
   Stores are required to submit daily operational reports for week number 6. These reports will track any anomalies, user feedback, and performance metrics for Nirvana Dock Pro.

4. **Access Restriction**  
   Product handling and technical operations are limited to authorized personnel within Cloud Operations and designated store staff under strict supervision.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Cloud Operations Team**  
  Coordinate product distribution, oversee installation, and monitor system status for Nirvana Dock Pro units in Texas.

- **Store Managers (Texas Region)**  
  Facilitate receipt, secure storage, and report submission related to the 81 units during week number 6.

- **Inventory Control Staff**  
  Ensure accurate logging of sample units tagged to NRG-ACC-2001 and maintain physical count integrity.

- **Support Desk**  
  Address technical queries and escalate any unresolved issues promptly according to escalation protocols.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Any discrepancies found during the logging or installation process, including sample unit shortages or damaged products, must be reported immediately to the Cloud Operations regional lead.

- Escalation path for unresolved issues:  
  1. Store Manager  
  2. Cloud Operations Regional Lead
