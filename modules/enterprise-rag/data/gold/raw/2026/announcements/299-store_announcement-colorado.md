## Controls

To preserve the information security and physical condition of the Nirvana Flex 13 devices, the following controls apply:

- Sample units must be stored in locked display cases outside of business hours.  
- Access to the sample units is restricted to authorized store personnel only.  
- All interactions with sample units must be logged in the store’s asset management system daily.  
- Any suspicious activity related to the sample units must be immediately reported to Information Security.  
- Software on sample units is updated weekly and monitored for unauthorized installations or access attempts.  
- Physical inventory checks will occur at least once per week starting week number 40.  

Failure to comply with these controls will result in escalation per the Exceptions and Escalation section.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to these controls can only be granted by the Information Security director and must be documented with a clear business justification. All exceptions require compensating controls such as increased monitoring or additional physical security.

- The Information Security Helpdesk
