## Executive Summary

This announcement from the Information Security department addresses key updates related to the Nirvana Dock Pro (product code **NRG-ACC-2001**) deployed in the Georgia region. Effective from June 9, 2026, stores will be implementing newly enhanced security measures that respond to findings gathered during week number **52** and based on a sample size of **107** units tested. These actions are critical to maintaining the integrity and security of retail operations and protecting Nirvana Retail Group’s assets and customer data.

The product Nirvana Dock Pro is a critical accessory in our retail ecosystem. Recent inspections and security assessments have highlighted areas where device firmware updates and additional configuration controls are necessary to mitigate risks related to unauthorized access and data leakage.

## Controls

- All Georgia stores must ensure that Nirvana Dock Pro devices are updated with the latest firmware by the end of week **52** as per Information Security directives.
- Devices must be registered and tracked with updated inventory records to ensure the total number of operational units aligns with the sample count of **107** tested units.
- Physical access to the devices must be restricted to authorized personnel only.
- Periodic security audits will be conducted to verify compliance with these controls and escalate any discrepancies immediately.
  
Example: If a store identifies any device anomalies or missing updates in Nirvana Dock Pro units, these must be reported within 24 hours to Information Security.

Any exceptions to this announcement must be formally requested through the Information Security department and approved prior to implementation. Immediate escalation is required for:

- Failure to update the Nirvana Dock Pro firmware by the deadline.
- Discrepancies noted in the accounting of sample units that deviate from the expected **107** tested units.

Escalations should be directed to the Georgia Regional Security Lead and the Information Security Incident Response Team, following the escalation matrix provided in the internal security policies.
