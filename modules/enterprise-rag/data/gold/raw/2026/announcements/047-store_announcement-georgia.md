# Information Security Store Announcement — Georgia #47

## Executive Summary

This announcement, document ID NRG-ANN-GOLD-0047, issued by the Information Security department and effective June 21, 2026, is directed exclusively to the Georgia region. It focuses on the security protocols related to the new shipment of the Nirvana Flex 13 laptop (product code NRG-LAP-1003). A total of 87 sample units have been deployed across stores in week number 48. All staff must adhere strictly to the updated controls and procedures to ensure these assets remain secure.

## Summary

The Information Security department announces the rollout of new information security measures for the Nirvana Flex 13 product line (NRG-LAP-1003) across all Georgia retail locations. This announcement corresponds with the delivery of 87 sample units arriving in week number 48. The measures outlined in this plan are to be implemented immediately to protect against risks such as theft, unauthorized access, and data compromise. The announcement is approved and applies exclusively to the Georgia region.

The Nirvana Flex 13 units are equipped with advanced security features which require store personnel to follow strict handling and access policies. Physical security and cybersecurity protocols must be enforced rigorously during all phases: receipt, display, storage, and customer interactions.

## Controls

- **Inventory Tracking:** All 87 sample units must be logged into the store inventory management system immediately upon receipt.  
- **Access Restriction:** Only authorized personnel in Georgia stores may handle or operate Nirvana Flex 13 models. Access credentials must be verified daily.  
- **Physical Security:** Units must be secured in locked display cases when stores are closed. Surveillance cameras should remain active 24/7 during high-risk hours.  
- **Data Protection:** All demo units must be reset to factory settings after customer use to prevent data leaks. Devices must be connected only to secured networks.  
- **Incident Reporting:** Any loss, theft, or suspected compromise of these units must be reported to the Information Security department within 1 hour of discovery.
