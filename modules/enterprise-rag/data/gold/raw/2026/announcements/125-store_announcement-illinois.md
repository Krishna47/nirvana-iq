## Executive Summary

This announcement outlines specific compliance requirements and operational controls for the product Nirvana Dock Pro (product code **NRG-ACC-2001**) across all Illinois region stores during week number **22**. It details sample unit expectations, compliance checks, and escalation procedures to maintain product integrity and regulatory adherence. ## Summary

This document, identified as **NRG-ANN-GOLD-0125**, applies exclusively to the Illinois region. It mandates a compliance verification process involving a sample size of **85** units of the Nirvana Dock Pro (product code **NRG-ACC-2001**). The aim is to ensure product quality and regulatory conformity within all applicable retail locations in Illinois during the specified week number **22**.

## Details

- **Product Name:** Nirvana Dock Pro

In week 22, stores in Illinois are required to conduct compliance audits on the Nirvana Dock Pro inventory by selecting a statistically valid sample of 85 units. This examination will assess packaging integrity, labeling accuracy, safety seal presence, and overall condition as per corporate and regulatory standards.

## Controls

- Sampling must be random and cover multiple store locations to ensure representativeness.  
- Each unit sampled must be recorded with product code **NRG-ACC-2001** for traceability.  
- Units failing compliance checks must be quarantined immediately and reported to Compliance.  
- All sample results must be documented using the standard compliance reporting tool by end of week 22.  
- Any discrepancies in product specifications or labeling must be escalated following the procedures outlined below.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Store Managers:** Oversee sampling process, ensure random selection of the 85 units, and coordinate with Compliance.  
- **Compliance Officers:** Review compliance reports, validate sampling integrity, and verify corrective actions.  
- **Inventory Teams:** Assist in identifying and isolating non-compliant units during the sample process.  
- **Regional Compliance Lead:** Consolidate data across stores in Illinois and present findings to Corporate Compliance.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- If the required sample size of 85 units cannot be met due to inventory shortages, the Store Manager must notify the Regional Compliance Lead immediately for approval of adjusted sampling procedures.  
- All compliance failures or discrepancies must be escalated to the Compliance department within 24 hours of discovery.  
- In cases of repeated non-compliance, stores may be subject to additional audits or operational restrictions until corrective actions are verified.

## Review Cycle

- Compliance feedback and audit results from week 22 will inform process improvements.
