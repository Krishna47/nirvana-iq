## Controls

Stores in the Florida region must adhere to the following controls for the Nirvana Air Purifier product line (NRG-HOM-2002):

- **Inventory Verification:** Confirm that each delivered unit matches recorded identifiers during shipment receipt.
- **Secure Storage:** Maintain the units in locked, monitored storage areas when not on the sales floor.
- **Access Control:** Limit physical handling of units to authorized personnel trained in security protocols.
- **Device Inspection:** Perform security checks on sample units weekly, focusing on firmware integrity and tamper evidence.
- **Incident Reporting:** Immediately report any anomalies or suspected tampering to the Information Security department via established escalation channels.
- **Data Handling:** Ensure any customer data collected via the device interface complies with company-wide privacy policies.
