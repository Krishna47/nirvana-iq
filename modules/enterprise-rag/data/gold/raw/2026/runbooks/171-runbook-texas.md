## Controls

- **Data Accuracy:** All financial inputs related to sample units must be validated by a secondary review within the Finance team before submission.  
- **Segregation of Duties:** Individuals responsible for recording financial transactions (sample unit recording) must not have reconciliation responsibilities to prevent fraud.  
- **Access Controls:** Only authorized Finance personnel within the Texas office may access detailed sample unit financial data to maintain confidentiality.  
- **Audit Trail:** Every entry and change related to the 91 sample units for product NRG-LAP-1001 will be logged with timestamps and user identification.
