## Controls

1. **Access Management:**  
   - Enforce least privilege access for all personnel handling Nirvana One Phone security tasks.  
   - Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for access to administrative consoles managing product firmware updates.

2. **Sampling and Testing:**  
   - Each week (notably week number **4**), security teams shall perform randomized sampling of **119 sample units** of the Nirvana One Phone to verify device integrity and monitor compliance with security policies.  
   - Sampling results must be logged and reviewed to detect anomalies such as unauthorized software, firmware tampering, or connectivity to untrusted networks.

3. **Incident Detection and Response:**  
   - Monitor alert thresholds based on anomalies detected during sample testing.  
   - Initiate incident response protocols immediately upon detection of any indicators of compromise involving Nirvana One Phone units.

4. **Audit and Compliance Tracking:**  
   - Maintain comprehensive logs of all access and changes to the Nirvana One Phone environment.  
   - Conduct quarterly security audits aligned with corporate policy for the Colorado region.
