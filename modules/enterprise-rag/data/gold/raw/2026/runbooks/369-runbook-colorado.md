## Controls

- **Access Control:** Only authorized personnel from the Information Security department and designated Colorado regional staff shall access sample units and associated data.  
- **Data Encryption:** All data related to sample analysis and reporting must be encrypted at rest and in transit following company encryption standards.  
- **Audit Logging:** Comprehensive logs of access, modification, and transfer events involving the 89 sample units must be maintained and reviewed weekly.  
- **Incident Response:** Immediate reporting of any security incidents related to the product code NRG-LAP-1001 is mandatory to the Colorado Security Operations Center (SOC).  
- **Physical Security:** Sample units must be stored in secured, access-controlled environments compliant with regional Colorado facility safeguards.  
- **Periodic Review:** Security measures must be validated at the conclusion of week number 6 and updated if vulnerabilities are detected.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Information Security Team (Colorado):** Execute runbook procedures, enforce access controls, and monitor security events around the Nirvana Air 14 samples.  
- **Regional Operations Manager (Colorado):** Coordinate logistics and facilitate compliance during sample collection and testing operations.  
- **Security Operations Center (SOC):** Manage incident response and investigate any reported breaches concerning product code NRG-LAP-1001.  
- **Compliance Officer:** Ensure that runbook processes align with corporate policies and regulatory requirements applicable to the Colorado region.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- Exceptions to any control must be formally documented and approved by the Information Security Director for Colorado.  
- Unresolved security issues or incidents beyond the scope of onsite personnel must be escalated immediately to the Nirvana Retail Group’s Corporate Security Leadership.  
- In the event of a suspected compromise involving the 89 sample units or during the week number 6 operations, escalation to the SOC is compulsory within one hour of detection.
