## Controls

- **Sample Unit Control:** Strictly limit samples to **96 units** per store for the specified week to prevent depletion of sellable inventory.  
- **Compliance Monitoring:** Region merchandising leads will monitor adherence weekly during store visits.  
- **Inventory Reconciliation:** Daily stock checks to reconcile sampled units versus sales to detect discrepancies or misuse.  
- **Reporting:** Mandatory submission of merchandising activity reports to the Merchandising department at the close of **week number 33**.  
- **Display Standards:** All Nirvana Pro 15 displays must follow standardized visual merchandising guidelines prescribed by the Merchandising department to maintain brand consistency.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Merchandising Managers (Washington):** Oversee implementation and weekly monitoring of the merchandising plan for NRG-LAP-1002.  
- **Store Managers:** Ensure sample units do not exceed the **96 unit** limit and that all displays conform to the manual.  
- **Inventory Controllers:** Track sample unit usage and report any discrepancies immediately to the Merchandising department.  
- **Sales Associates:** Engage customers by demonstrating product features without compromising sample control.  
- **Regional Director of Merchandising:** Approves escalations and exceptions related to the merchandising execution.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- **Exceptions:** Any requirement to exceed the **96 sample units** allocation must be justified with a documented business case and approved by the Regional Director of Merchandising.  
- **Escalation Procedures:** Non-compliance or incidents regarding merchandising controls shall be reported within 24 hours to the Merchandising Managers, who will escalate unresolved issues to regional leadership.  
- **Risk Mitigation:** Unauthorized sample overuse risks inventory shortages, loss of sales, and brand inconsistency.
