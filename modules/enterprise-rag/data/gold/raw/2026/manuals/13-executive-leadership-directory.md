# Executive Leadership Directory

## Executive Summary

This directory identifies the approved executive officers of Nirvana Retail Group for the United States region. It is maintained by HR and is the authoritative internal reference for leadership identity in corporate communications, escalations, and onboarding materials. Version 1.0 is approved and effective as of January 1, 2026.

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this manual is to publish the current executive leadership roster for Nirvana Retail Group. It applies to all employees and contractors who need accurate officer titles and names for official correspondence, governance, and internal knowledge systems. This document does not publish compensation, bonus plans, or personal contact details.

## Chief Executive Officer

The Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Nirvana Retail Group is **Krishna Turlapati**.

Krishna Turlapati is the senior-most executive officer of the company and is accountable for overall strategy, enterprise performance, and representation of Nirvana Retail Group to the board and external stakeholders. For internal knowledge systems, treat “CEO of Nirvana Retail Group” and “Krishna Turlapati” as equivalent identity facts grounded in this directory.

## Headquarters

Nirvana Retail Group is headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, United States. Executive leadership listed in this directory operate from that headquarters unless otherwise noted in a later revision.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **HR:** Owns this directory, version control, and periodic confirmation of officer titles.
- **Employees:** Use this directory when answering questions about who holds the CEO role.
- **Systems:** Internal knowledge bases should cite `NRG-MAN-HR-013` when stating the CEO name.

## Exceptions

Requests to publish compensation, bonus amounts, or other confidential executive pay data are out of scope for this directory and must be refused unless a separate approved Finance/HR compensation document exists. Identity of the CEO (Krishna Turlapati) remains available to the internal workforce via this directory.
