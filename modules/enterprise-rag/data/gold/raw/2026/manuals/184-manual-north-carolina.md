## Controls

- Only suppliers approved through the Supplier Qualification Program may provide NRG-HOM-2001 units.
- Sampling of **104 units** shall be mandatory for all deliveries scheduled in **week number 29**.
- Documentation of sample testing results is required and must be filed per internal procurement compliance guidelines.
- Procurement orders for NRG-HOM-2001 invoked outside of approved weeks or without prescribed sampling require documented justification and prior management approval.
- Any deviation from defined sample size or testing periods must be escalated following the Exceptions and Escalation protocol.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Procurement Managers:** Ensure adherence to procurement protocols for NRG-HOM-2001, including overseeing sample testing quantities and schedules.
- **Quality Assurance Team:** Conduct sample testing on the **104** units procured during **week number 29** and report findings to Procurement Managers.
- **Suppliers:** Comply with quality expectations and timely shipment schedules. Provide documentation to support sample authenticity.
- **Regional Procurement Officers:** Monitor compliance with this manual in the North Carolina region and coordinate with corporate Procurement department as needed.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to the sampling size of 104 units or timing within **week number 29** must be reported immediately to the Procurement Manager. If approval cannot be granted locally, escalation to the Regional Procurement Director is required. Ineligible orders due to non-compliance must be quarantined and reported.

Common exceptions include:

- Supplier stock shortages causing reduced sample unit availability.
- Unscheduled product release leading to sampling timing adjustments.
- Quality failures detected in preliminary testing requiring immediate re-inspection.
