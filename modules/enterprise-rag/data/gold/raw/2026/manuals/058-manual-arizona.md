## Controls

To maintain procurement quality for NRG-HOM-2001, the following controls are mandated:

- **Sample Size Control:** Exactly 98 units must be sampled per audit cycle in week number 7. Sampling fewer units requires documented approval from procurement leadership.  
- **Supplier Certification:** All suppliers must maintain valid certifications and have passed prior quality audits no older than six months.  
- **Compliance Verification:** All procured ovens must comply with regional safety and performance standards specific to Arizona regulations.  
- **Documentation:** Procuring officers must log each batch’s sample results in the procurement system within 48 hours post sampling.  
- **Revision Control:** Only version 1.0 of this manual is valid until official updates are published and approved.  

Failures to comply with any control trigger immediate review and potential supplier reassessment to mitigate risk to product quality and company reputation.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Procurement Officers:** Execute sampling of 98 units per cycle, ensure documentation is accurate, and report findings.  
- **Procurement Managers:** Review sampling reports, approve exceptions, and maintain supplier relationships and certifications.  
- **Quality Assurance Team:** Collaborate on audit design and perform independent spot checks when escalated.  
- **Regional Compliance Officer:** Monitor adherence to Arizona region-specific regulations involved in procurement activities.  

Clear delineation of responsibilities ensures accountability and adherence to this manual’s standards.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- **Exceptions:** Any deviations from the required 98 sample units must be formally documented with rationale and approved by the Procurement Manager prior to execution.  
- **Escalation:** If sampling results show non-compliance or adverse trends, the issue must be escalated immediately to the Regional Compliance Officer for investigation.  
- **Supplier Non-Conformance:** Repeated failure in supplier quality audits will trigger a supplier review board that may result in probation or discontinuation.  

Rapid and documented handling of exceptions protects product integrity and minimizes operational risks.
