# Merchandising Manual — New York #52

## Executive Summary

This manual, identified as **NRG-MAN-GOLD-0052**, outlines the merchandising processes and standards for the **New York** region’s deployment of the **Nirvana Vision 55** television, product code **NRG-TV-4001**. The procedures detailed herein are intended to optimize product presentation starting in **week_number 1** of the merchandising cycle, using a sample size of **92** units for display and customer engagement studies. This document is currently **approved** for use and represents version **1.0** of the manual.

## Summary

This manual serves the **Merchandising** department within the **New York** region and governs the handling, display, and quality assurance of the Nirvana Vision 55 TV (product code **NRG-TV-4001**). The primary objective is to ensure consistent merchandising practices from initial product placement, through the first week of release and maintain adherence to company standards to maximize customer experience and sales performance.

Key parameters include a sample unit count of **92** for pilot merchandising displays conducted in week **1** of deployment. The manual outlines responsibilities, controls, and escalation protocols designed to support efficient cycle management.

## Details

- **Product Name:** Nirvana Vision 55  
- **Product Code:** NRG-TV-4001  
- **Region:** New York  
- **Department:** Merchandising  
- **Sample Units:** 92  
- **Week Number:** 1  

Merchandising teams must ensure that all **92** sample units of the Nirvana Vision 55 are displayed according to layout specifications aligned with local store configurations. The displays will serve both as demonstration units for customers and for data collection on product interaction during the initial merchandising week.

All in-store staff involved in merchandising will be trained on the unique features of the Nirvana Vision 55, including setup, troubleshooting, and customer engagement techniques. Inventory reconciliation linked to the sample units must be performed daily during week **1** to track stock movements and return damaged or unsellable units promptly.

## Controls

- All sample units (**92**) must be accounted for daily and reconciled against inventory management systems.  
- Display layouts are standardized and must be approved by the regional merchandising manager before deployment.  
- Product code **NRG-TV-4001** must be clearly tagged on all merchandising materials and POS displays.  
- Weekly audit checks on merchandising status and stock integrity to ensure compliance throughout week **1**.  
- Immediate reporting is required in cases where display sample units are damaged, missing, or do not meet presentation standards.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Regional Merchandising Manager**: Approves final display layouts and oversees compliance.  
- **Store Merchandisers**: Responsible for accurate setup and upkeep of Nirvana Vision 55 displays using the **92** sample units.  
- **Inventory Control Team**: Performs daily stock checks and reports discrepancies.  
- **Training Coordinator**: Ensures all merchandising and sales staff understand product features and merchandising standards.

## Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to prescribed sample unit counts or display layouts must be authorized in writing by the Regional Merchandising Manager.  
Escalate the following issues immediately to senior management:  
- Shortage of sample units beyond a threshold of 5% of the **92** units.  
- Significant deviations in product presentation quality.  
- Unresolved inventory discrepancies after 24 hours.
