## Summary

NRG-SOP-GOLD-0181 provides detailed steps and controls to maintain compliance for the **Nirvana Pro 15** product in **Texas**. This procedure is mandatory for all Compliance personnel operating within the Texas region.

## Purpose and Scope

This document establishes standardized compliance procedures for monitoring and verifying the product **NRG-LAP-1002 — Nirvana Pro 15** in the state of **Texas**. It applies to all employees and contractors within the Compliance department responsible for regulatory adherence, product quality sampling, and reporting in that region.

The scope covers:

- Sampling methodology during week 26 each year.
- Documentation and reporting protocols.
- Escalation procedures for compliance deviations.

## Prerequisites

- Access to product batch and distribution records for **NRG-LAP-1002**.
- Training on Compliance protocols for Texas regulations.
- Sampling kits prepared for collecting **101** units.
- Permission from Compliance management for scheduled audits.

## Procedure Steps

1. Verify current production batches of **Nirvana Pro 15** (product code **NRG-LAP-1002**) in Texas distribution channels during week 26.
2. Collect a statistically significant sample of **101** units from various retail points.
3. Conduct physical inspections to confirm compliance with Texas safety and quality standards.
4. Document all sample testing results in compliance tracking systems.
5. Report findings to the Compliance department and escalate any out-of-spec results promptly.
6. Retain records as per company document retention policy.

## Controls

- Sampling must only be performed during week number **26** to ensure periodic compliance verification.
- Sample size fixed at **101** units, neither fewer nor greater, to maintain statistical validity.
- Use approved sampling tools and maintain chain-of-custody documentation.
- Include cross-checks by a secondary Compliance officer before final approval.

## Controls and Metrics

- Compliance rate to be monitored monthly with a target of 100% conformance for sampled units.
- Non-compliance occurrences must not exceed 2% of total samples; investigations triggered otherwise.
- Weekly reports generated post-sampling, including any deviations, corrective actions, and resolutions.
- All findings recorded under document ID **NRG-SOP-GOLD-0181** for traceability.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Compliance Officers:** Execute sampling, perform tests, and record findings.
- **Compliance Managers:** Authorize sampling schedules and verify final documentation.
- **Quality Assurance Team:** Support testing efforts and validate results.
- **Regional Director — Texas:** Ensure alignment of resources and overall procedure compliance.

## Exceptions and Escalation

- If insufficient sample units (**less than 101**) are available due to supply constraints, escalate immediately to the Compliance Manager.
- Deviations from procedure timing (sampling outside week 26) require documented approval from the department head.
- Critical non-conformances must be escalated to the Regional Director within 24 hours.
