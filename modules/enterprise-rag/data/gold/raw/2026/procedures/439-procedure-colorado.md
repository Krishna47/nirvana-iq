## Summary
NRG-SOP-GOLD-0439 outlines operational steps to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Nirvana Retail Group’s information assets related to the product Nirvana Vision 55 (NRG-TV-4001) in the Colorado region. It specifies procedures to detect and respond to security incidents, applying region-specific controls and metrics to maintain enterprise security standards.

## Purpose and Scope
The purpose of this document is to provide standardized information security procedures for the handling, monitoring, and protection of Nirvana Vision 55 (product code NRG-TV-4001) within the Colorado region. This procedure applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties involved with or having access to these products and related information systems.

## Prerequisites
- Completion of foundational security training by all personnel in the Information Security department.
- Deployment of approved security tools and endpoint protection on all relevant devices.
- Up-to-date asset inventory inclusive of 119 sampled units of Nirvana Vision 55 during week_number 24.
- Access control lists (ACLs) and user permission reviews completed in advance.

## Details
The Nirvana Vision 55 (NRG-TV-4001) products in circulation within the Colorado region require systematic security reviews. During week_number 24, a statistically determined sample of 119 units will undergo in-depth security evaluation to detect vulnerabilities. These findings will feed into risk mitigation plans coordinated by the Information Security department.

## Procedure Steps
1. **Sample Selection**  
   Select 119 units from the inventory managed within the Colorado region according to serial number and location distribution.
   
2. **Security Assessment**  
   Conduct vulnerability scans and firmware integrity checks on the selected Nirvana Vision 55 units.
   
3. **Incident Identification**  
   Document and report any anomalies or security breaches encountered during assessments.
   
4. **Mitigation Actions**  
   Apply patches or configuration changes to affected units as prescribed by the Information Security team.
   
5. **Documentation**  
   Record all findings and remediation steps in the centralized incident management system.
   
6. **Reporting**  
   Submit a weekly status summary to the department head and regional management for review.

## Controls
- Restrict physical and network access to Nirvana Vision 55 units based on role-based access control.
- Mandatory encryption for all data stored on and transmitted by product units.
- Routine patch management and firmware updates aligned with enterprise schedules.
- Continuous monitoring of security events specific to product code NRG-TV-4001.

## Controls and Metrics
- Compliance with the sampling target of 119 units during week_number 24 to ensure representative security coverage.
- Track time-to-resolution for detected vulnerabilities on Nirvana Vision 55 products.
- Measure the frequency and severity of incidents within the Colorado region.
- Conduct quarterly audits of adherence to access control procedures.

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Information Security Analysts:** Execute security assessments on selected product units; report anomalies.
- **Security Incident Response Team:** Lead mitigation and investigation of security breaches.
- **Regional Security Manager (Colorado):** Oversee procedural adherence and approve remedial actions.
- **Asset Management Team:** Maintain accurate inventory records including the 119 sampled Nirvana Vision 55 units.

## Exceptions and Escalation
- Exceptions to this procedure must be approved in writing by the Regional Security Manager. 
- Any critical or unresolved incidents detected during sample assessments must be escalated immediately to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and the department director.
- Failure to comply with controls may result in disciplinary action or revocation of access.
