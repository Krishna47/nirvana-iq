# Cloud Operations Procedure — Colorado #259

## Executive Summary

This document, **NRG-SOP-GOLD-0259**, defines the standardized Cloud Operations Procedure for the **Colorado** region to support the product **Nirvana Vision 55** (product code **NRG-TV-4001**). It establishes the workflow, controls, and metrics to be followed during operational activities, ensuring consistent service quality while managing a sample unit size of **99** per operation cycle at **week number 52**. This version **1.0** is approved and effective as of **2026-02-17**.

---

## Summary

The Cloud Operations Procedure detailed herein applies specifically to the **Colorado** region within Nirvana Retail Group’s Cloud Operations department. It describes the step-by-step process and quality controls necessary to maintain and monitor the cloud infrastructure that supports the **Nirvana Vision 55** television product, identified with product code **NRG-TV-4001**.

---

## Purpose and Scope

The purpose of this procedure is to standardize cloud service operations related to **Nirvana Vision 55** deployments in the **Colorado** region, ensuring high availability and performance. The scope includes monitoring, maintenance, sample testing, incident escalation, and documentation as related to cloud environments supporting product code **NRG-TV-4001** with a specific focus on operations conducted in **week number 52** involving **99** sample units.

---

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Cloud Operations platforms with appropriate credentials.
- Active awareness of the product lifecycle for **NRG-TV-4001**.
- Training on Nirvana Retail Group’s cloud monitoring tools.
- Understanding of regional compliance requirements for **Colorado**.
- Availability of sample units totaling **99** for operational checks.

---

## Procedure Steps

1. **Preparation**  
   a. Confirm availability of **99** sample units for testing during **week number 52**.  
   b. Verify system access and credentials for the **Colorado** cloud environment.

2. **Monitoring**  
   a. Initiate health checks on cloud resources related to **NRG-TV-4001** deployments.  
   b. Collect performance metrics and verify against baseline KPIs.

3. **Sample Testing**  
   a. Execute integrity and responsiveness tests on the **99** sample units.  
   b. Record anomalies or deviations for further analysis.

4. **Incident Handling**  
   a. Identify and categorize incidents based on severity.  
   b. Escalate unresolved or critical incidents per escalation protocol.

5. **Documentation**  
   a. Log all operational activities, results, and communications.  
   b. Submit daily status reports to Cloud Operations management.

---

## Controls

- Mandatory logging of all cloud operation activities to the central incident management system.
- Verification of sample integrity for the **99** units before and after operations.
- Periodic audits conducted on cloud configurations specific to **NRG-TV-4001**.
- Adherence to change management policies during updates or incident mitigation.

---

## Controls and Metrics

- Sample size: **99** units tested weekly at **week number 52** to ensure statistical validity.
- Availability metric: Cloud resource uptime must meet or exceed 99.95%.
- Incident response time: Critical incidents escalated within 30 minutes.
- Baseline performance metrics maintained for **NRG-TV-4001** monitored continuously.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Cloud Operations Team**: Execute procedure steps, monitor cloud infrastructure, and conduct sample testing for **Nirvana Vision 55** in **Colorado**.  
- **Regional Cloud Manager (Colorado)**: Approve operational plans, oversee escalation, and ensure compliance with regional policies.  
- **Quality Assurance**: Validate result accuracy from sample units and audit operational consistency.

---

## Exceptions and Escalation

- If sample units fall below **99** due to unavailability, immediately notify Regional Cloud Manager for approval to proceed or reschedule.  
- Any critical incident unresolved within 30 minutes must be escalated to the Cloud Operations Director.  
- Exceptions to procedure steps require documented management approval and must be logged in the incident tracking system.

---

## Review Cycle

This procedure shall be reviewed annually or upon significant changes to cloud infrastructure or the **NRG-TV-4001** product line, whichever occurs first. The next review is scheduled for February 2027.
